creature_pk_vfreq_3_common

*The purpose of these files are to change the frequencies of all vanilla animals to meet my own standards.*
*This file is for critters who are normal creatures found everywhere*
*Good Candidates: large packs of small animals, small packs of prey animals, small predators, something that is present in small variations (snow fox versus red tail fox, trout versus salmon, etc)*
*default is 60 freq*
